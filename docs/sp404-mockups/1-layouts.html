SP-404 MK2 editor: desk layouts

FX on top, pad mixer below, MX-1 cues. Window drawn at 1600 × 980. Pads are grouped by bank, and empty pads are hidden (here: A 12 pads, B 5, C 8, D 3 = 28 strips plus 4 bank masters). The bank colour runs across each group's title. Click KEYBOARD in the top bar to see the Launchkey panel slide over the mixer.
Strip, top to bottom (follows the signal flow sample → envelope → level → bank volume → bus): pad label in bank colour · MIDI note and channel (from the app's MIDI Mode / note offset setting) · sample name (LCD) · AHR envelope thumbnail (click to edit Attack / Hold / Release) · mute group (– or A–J) · routing DRY / BUS 1 / BUS 2, in the device colours (white / orange / green) · pan knob (L50–R50) · MUTE (app-side: level to 0 and back) · level fader 0–127 · value.
Bank master (black label, right of each group): bank volume 0–127, which scales the whole bank on the device. A white line on top of a strip = that pad is sounding (status poll); a lighter strip = the pad selected on the unit.
